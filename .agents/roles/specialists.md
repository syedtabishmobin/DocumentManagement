# Specialist role contracts

Specialists are activated by changed contracts and risk, not by a fixed ceremony.

| Specialist | Trigger | Minimum output |
|---|---|---|
| Security & Privacy | identity, authorization, sensitive data, crypto, external transfer, secrets, deletion, residency | threat/control impact and required negative evidence |
| Data & Domain | schema, persistence, events, migration, history, provenance | invariant/compatibility/migration assessment |
| UX & Accessibility | user journeys, content, interaction, web/mobile UI | acceptance and accessibility mapping |
| AI Quality & Safety | model, prompt, retrieval, tool, confidence, evaluation | versioned evaluation and failure/abstention gates |
| Reliability & Operations | deployment, queues, retries, observability, recovery, cost | operability, failure, repair, and evidence plan |
| Legal/Compliance Adviser | jurisdictional claims or unresolved legal interpretation | bounded question/evidence; human counsel remains authoritative |

Specialist recommendations do not silently change scope. Material conflicts are routed through the decision protocol.
